About the Firm

Sentry Analytics went into business in June 2026, founded by Wyatt Richards, Colby Gustafson and Jacob McCarthy. Clients include small to mid-sized businesses, private equity firms and their portfolio companies, and healthcare organizations.

Since inception, Sentry has handled

  • Integrated AI assistants built front end to back endQuoting, answering questions and building reports from live client data, with guardrails built in.
  • Cloud data platformsBuilt in Azure from scratch, and running in production daily.
  • Backend and ERP overhaulsMaster data, legacy records and reporting databases rebuilt.
  • CRM and ERP reverse engineeringUndocumented systems mapped so their data can be put to use.
  • Automated financial reportingMonthly closes rebuilt in code and matched to the penny.
  • Dashboards and analyticsPower BI and SQL reporting on protected health data.

Most projects start with siloed data spread across spreadsheets, legacy systems, ERPs, and a handful of other systems. Sentry brings it into one place, makes it reliable, and then builds the reporting, automation and AI tools the business needs on top of it. The partners do the work themselves.

This work commonly involves sensitive and protected health information, financial records and customer data, so access, credentials and security are critically prioritized in every effort.

Services

Single projects and ongoing work across the areas below.

Data Analytics

Dashboards, recurring reports and one-off analysis in Power BI, SQL, Python and Excel. This also covers financial reporting, forecasting, pricing and inventory analysis, and helping a team decide which numbers to track.

Data Engineering

Databases, pipelines and integrations that bring data from spreadsheets, file feeds and separate systems into one place. Built in the cloud, usually on Azure, or on the servers a company already runs.

AI Tools

Custom AI assistants built on a company's own data, from the chat window a customer sees to the back end behind it. Examples include a quoting assistant for customers, an internal assistant that knows the company's products and resources, and tools that write reports or pull data out of documents.

Backend Redesign

Rebuilding the systems behind a business when they no longer keep up. That can mean overhauling the master data in an ERP, replacing a spreadsheet process with a real database, or moving a manual operation onto a cloud platform.

Systems Integration

Connecting ERPs, CRMs, storefronts and file feeds that were never set up to share data. Undocumented systems are reverse engineered and their data mapped directly.

Automation

Recurring manual work set up to run on a schedule. Monthly reports, data entry between systems, reconciliations, file handling and collecting data from the web are the usual candidates.

Web and Front End

The screens people use, with the back end behind them. That covers chat interfaces, internal tools, company websites and storefront work on platforms like Shopify.

Security and Access

Access controls, credentials, backups and security reviews for new builds and the systems a company already runs, including databases that hold protected health information.

Strategy and Assessments

A full written review of the data and systems a company has today, for when the assessment itself is the deliverable. It is useful during diligence or after an acquisition, or when deciding where AI fits.

How Engagements Work

Every engagement starts with a free due diligence period. Billing starts only once the scope is confirmed, and from there the work is billed by the hour.

  1. 01No charge

    Free Due Diligence

    A review of the data and systems the business has today, and a conversation about what it needs. There is no charge for this period.

  2. 02No charge

    Confirmed Scope

    A written action plan with an hour estimate, adjusted until both sides agree on the scope. Billing starts only after that.

  3. 03Billing starts

    Build

    Work is billed hourly against the agreed scope. A detailed timesheet goes out every month, so each hour can be traced to something specific.

  4. 04As needed

    Handoff and Support

    Everything built is documented and belongs to the client. After delivery, support continues by the hour, or the work is handed off to the client's own team.
